## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an outdoor angling workbench and hydrographic data platform. The brand personality balances field precision, ecological quietude, and scientific observation. It speaks to anglers who regard fishing not as a gamble, but as a deliberate discipline of recording, analyzing, and deciphering currents, barometric pressure, water temperature, and bite patterns.

The visual aesthetic synthesizes **Tactile Field Instrumentation** with **Contemporary Minimalist Workbenches**. It firmly avoids synthetic neon gradients, glossy glass effects, and generic gaming gamification. Instead, surfaces echo wet river stones, matte carbon-fiber rods, topo-bathymetric surveys, and waterproof field notebooks. Visual elements carry crisp hairline divisions, precise pinheads, stamped measurement badges, and high-visibility beacon accents derived directly from fishing floats.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the chromatic realities of water bodies and lakeside field conditions under changing natural daylight:

- **Primary (`#24718A`, Lake Blue / Deep Teal):** Used for key navigation anchors, selected states, depth scales, and interactive status headers.
- **Secondary (`#1A4D5F`, Dark Cyan Blue):** Used for structural container backgrounds, bathymetric data groups, and dense telemetry toolbars.
- **Tertiary / Signal Accent (`#E65F2B`, Float Orange-Red):** High-alert, optical strike indicator color. Reserved exclusively for bite alarms, strike events, floating pointers, and live GPS recording pins.
- **Auxiliary Accent (`#D4A359`, Mist Gold):** Reflects dawn surface reflections and solar elevation; dedicated to lunar phases, golden hours, and master catch milestones.
- **Deep Hydrographic Surface (`#14323D`):** Deep-water night card background, contrast telemetry monitors, and primary dark text containers.
- **Neutral Grounds (`#F8FAFA` Paper Mist White, `#EBF3F5` Light Lake Wash, `#F4F6F8` Wet Pebble Grey):** Provide natural, low-glare outdoor legibility with zero visual fatigue under direct sunlight.

Semantic roles:
- **Surface Canvas:** `#F8FAFA`
- **Surface Card (Elevated):** `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface Muted / Well:** `#EBF3F5`
- **Surface Telemetry (Inverted):** `#14323D`
- **Border Subtle:** `rgba(26, 77, 95, 0.12)`
- **Border Structural:** `rgba(20, 50, 61, 0.22)`
- **Border Signal:** `#E65F2B`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy accommodates dual cognitive modes: rapid glanceability in bright outdoor conditions and high-density technical reading during log evaluations.

- **Display & Section Anchors (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers geometric precision and technical weight for screen headers, metric milestones, and point-of-interest labels.
- **Readout & Body Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, balanced, and humanist with exceptional legibility across dense field notes, tackle configuration logs, and hydrographic analysis paragraphs.
- **Scientific Telemetry & Metric Values (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to critical numerical figures—water temperature, barometric pressure (hPa), water flow velocity, GPS coordinates, depth meters, wind knots, and tackle sizes (#0.8, #2.5). Monospaced alignment guarantees zero optical jitter during real-time value updates.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system targets a specialized mobile viewport standard (390×844) structured around a dense, high-utility 4-column fluid mobile grid. 

- **Canvas Margins:** Fixed `16px` (`margin`) outer horizontal safe padding to maximize active real estate while preventing thumb edge clipping.
- **Column Gutters:** `12px` (`gutter`) standard gap between coordinate tiles, split telemetry pairs, and grid cards.
- **Rhythm Units:** Formulated on a strict 4px/8px module:
  - `space-xs` (4px): Chip internal vertical gaps, indicator dot offsets, stamp padding.
  - `space-sm` (8px): Metadata row spacing, icon-to-label separation, badge padding.
  - `space-md` (12px): Intra-card modular content grouping.
  - `space-lg` (16px): Card internal structural padding.
  - `space-xl` (24px): Logical section breaks between telemetry clusters and log histories.
- **Thumb Zone Compliance:** Primary field interactions (Strike Record, Mark Waypoint, Cast Counter) reside in the bottom 40% vertical viewport sweep, accessible via dedicated bottom-pinned instrument docks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated, diffuse dropshadows in favor of **Tonal Layering with Structural Hairlines and Tactile Inset Stamps**:

1. **Base Ground (Level 0):** `#F8FAFA` canvas with optional 5% opacity topographic bathymetric line overlays.
2. **Field Surface (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` cards bordered by a crisp `1px` low-contrast edge (`rgba(26, 77, 95, 0.12)`). No blur; zero float illusion.
3. **Telemetry Wells (Level 2 - Inset):** Recessed containers using `#EBF3F5` or dark basin `#14323D`, bound by `1px` structural borders (`rgba(20, 50, 61, 0.18)`), creating a dedicated digital gauge enclosure.
4. **Instrument Float (Level 3):** Modal overlays and bottom floating action modules use an ultra-crisp directional contact shadow: `0 4px 16px rgba(20, 50, 61, 0.12), 0 1px 2px rgba(20, 50, 61, 0.08)`, topped with a `1px` matte border.
5. **High-Visibility Overlays:** Live beacon pulses use sharp, solid outline rings (1.5px `#E65F2B`) rather than diffuse glowing drop-shadows, maintaining sharp visibility under sunlight glare.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects machined physical instruments, molded tackle storage boxes, and waterproof field logbooks. 

- The overall roundedness is anchored at level `1` (Soft): `4px` (`space-xs`) for structural inner elements and mini badges; `8px` (`rounded-lg`) for primary cards and modular instrument blocks; `12px` (`rounded-xl`) for sheet panels and modal viewports.
- Fully rounded pills (`9999px`) are strictly reserved for inline status indicators, floating strike buttons, and hook/lead configuration chips to maintain high tap-target ergonomics.
- Stamp corners and telemetry status badges may utilize diagonal chamfer accents (2px corner clips) in pure SVG implementations to reinforce laboratory field aesthetics.

## Components

### 1. Primary Action & Field Trigger Buttons
- **Strike Action Button:** Floating 56px circular or full-width pill button finished in intense Float Orange-Red (`#E65F2B`) with high-contrast white monospaced labeling (`label-data-md`). Tactile 1.5px border in darker amber `#BF4616`.
- **Standard Controls:** Outlined in 1px `#1A4D5F` with surface fill `#EBF3F5`, typography in `#14323D`, ensuring firm tactile affordance.

### 2. Rigging & Lure Chips (Tackle Tags)
- Miniature specification tags with solid `#FFFFFF` or `#EBF3F5` fills, bound by `1px` hairline boundaries.
- Uses `JetBrains Mono` at `10px` or `12px` to display exact parameters (e.g., `PE #1.2 / LEADER 16LB / SINKER 28g`).
- Left-side accent dot represents lead weight or rig classification.

### 3. Hydrographic & Telemetry Cards
- **Dark Bathymetric Block:** Background `#14323D` with text rendered in `#F8FAFA` and values in `#EBF3F5`. Top right corner carries real-time sync indicators in `#24718A` or `#D4A359`.
- **Daylight Metric Grid:** Dual-split or tri-split numeric tiles displaying water temperature (°C), atmospheric barometer curve (hPa), and lunar tide rhythm. Values utilize `label-data-lg` with unit labels in `label-caps`.

### 4. Catch Log & Waypoint Lists
- Compact row items separated by single-pixel hairline dividers (`rgba(26, 77, 95, 0.08)`).
- Left: Monospaced timestamp and GPS water depth. Center: Target species thumbnail and tackle rig label. Right: Specimen length/weight badge stamped with `#D4A359` borders.

### 5. Input Fields & Log Adjusters
- Grounded input containers in `#FFFFFF` with structural `1px` borders in `rgba(26, 77, 95, 0.20)`. Focus states switch crisp borders to `#24718A` with zero focus glow.
- Numeric counters (depth, drag rating, cast count) utilize segmented `+ / -` mechanical toggles with tactile press feedback states.

### 6. Interactive Map Pins & Float Beacons
- Vector map pins styled as physical plumb bobs or slender float markers.
- Features a center core marker with a concentric 1.5px ring radiating outwards to designate current casting targets and historical strike hotspots.
